⏱ Before & After Gym

30–90 min before
  • Light snack: banana + whey / buttermilk / fruit + nuts (easy to digest carbs + protein).
  • Drink 300–500 ml water; sip, do not chug.
  • Avoid heavy oily food to prevent discomfort during training.
Just before workout
  • 5–10 min dynamic warm-up (walking, arm circles, leg swings, light treadmill).
  • Mobility for tight areas: shoulders, hips, ankles.
  • Set intention: which lifts are priority today.
Immediately after
  • Cool down 5–10 min: slow walk + light stretching.
  • Rehydrate 500–750 ml water; use electrolytes if sweat was heavy.
  • Protein + carbs within 1 hour (whey + fruit / paneer + roti / egg bhurji + toast).
Recovery habits
  • Target 7–8 hours of sleep for better fat loss and muscle recovery.
  • On off-days: light walk + stretching instead of full rest on the sofa.
  • Notice soreness vs pain: joint pain = reduce weight or skip exercise.
This routine supports fat loss, keeps performance stable, and reduces injury risk by combining warm-up, structured training, and consistent recovery.

🥗 Diet Rules (Fat Loss)

Do this ✅
  • Protein every meal (eggs, chicken, fish, dal, paneer, curd).
  • Rice/roti allowed but portioned; fill half plate with veggies first.
  • Dinner before 9 PM; keep it lighter and high protein.
  • 3–3.5 litres water daily, more on intense cardio days.
Avoid ❌
  • Sugary drinks, desserts, packaged juices most days.
  • Fried snacks, bakery items, and frequent outside food.
  • Late-night heavy meals; stop mindless munching after dinner.
  • Alcohol; it slows fat loss and affects sleep quality./li>

🧠 Training Rules

  • Week 1: Use ~70% of your previous working weights, focus on clean form.
  • From Week 2: progressive overload – add small weight or 1–2 reps if it felt easy.
  • Consistency beats perfection: even a 30-min short session is better than a skip.
  • Keep most sets 1–2 reps away from total failure to recover better.
