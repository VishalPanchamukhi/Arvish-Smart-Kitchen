Quick Recipes

🥚 Perfect Omelette / Bhurji
  • Heat ½ tsp ghee in a non-stick pan.
  • Sauté chopped onion & chili for 1 min.
  • Pour whisked eggs (salt added). Cook on low flame.
🍗 Light Chicken Curry
  • Heat 1 tsp ghee. Add jeera, onion, ginger-garlic paste.
  • Add chicken pieces + turmeric + masala. Sauté 2 mins.
  • Add water, cover and cook till tender. No frying.
🧀 Paneer Stir Fry
  • Heat ½ tsp ghee.
  • Toss paneer cubes and capsicum slices on high heat for 2 mins.
  • Sprinkle salt & pepper. Serve warm.
🌱 Sprouts (Steamed)
  • Soak moong/chana overnight.
  • Pressure cook for 1 whistle OR steam for 10 mins.
  • Toss with raw onion, lemon juice, and salt.
