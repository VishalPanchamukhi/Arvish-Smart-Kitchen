Quick Recipes

🥚 Perfect Omelette / Bhurji
  • Beat 2 eggs with ¼ tsp salt and a pinch of pepper.
  • Finely chop 2 tbsp onion, 1 green chilli, and 1 tbsp coriander leaves.
  • Heat ½ tsp ghee in a non-stick tawa on medium flame.
  • For omelette: Sauté onion and chilli for 1 minute, pour egg, spread gently, cook till set, then fold.
  • For bhurji: Pour egg, keep stirring softly for 2–3 minutes till small soft pieces form, switch off before it dries.
🍗 Light Chicken Curry (South Indian)
  • Take 250 g chicken pieces, rinse and keep aside.
  • For masala: Slice 1 medium onion, crush 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, and chop 1 tomato.
  • Heat 1 tsp ghee in a kadai. Add ½ tsp jeera, 4–5 curry leaves, and let them splutter.
  • Add onion and sauté 3–4 minutes till light golden, then add ginger-garlic paste and fry till raw smell goes.
  • Add tomato, ¼ tsp turmeric, ½–¾ tsp red chilli powder, ½ tsp coriander powder, and salt; cook till tomato softens.
  • Add chicken, mix well with the masala and sauté for 3–4 minutes on medium flame.
  • Pour ¾–1 cup hot water for a light gravy, cover and cook 12–15 minutes till chicken is tender.
  • Finish with ½ tsp lemon juice and a few coriander leaves; keep gravy thin and avoid extra ghee.
🧀 Paneer Stir Fry (South Indian style)
  • Cut 100 g paneer into small cubes and slice ¼ cup capsicum and ¼ cup onion.
  • Heat ½ tsp ghee in a wide pan. Add ¼ tsp mustard seeds and 4–5 curry leaves.
  • Add onion and capsicum, sauté 2–3 minutes on medium flame without browning too much.
  • Add paneer cubes, sprinkle ¼ tsp turmeric, a pinch of red chilli powder, salt, and gently toss.
  • Cook just 2–3 minutes on low flame so paneer stays soft; finish with fresh coriander and serve immediately.
🌱 Steamed Sprouts Sundal
  • Soak ½ cup whole moong or chana overnight and drain.
  • Pressure cook with water and a pinch of salt for 1 whistle or steam 10–12 minutes till just soft.
  • Heat ½ tsp oil or ghee in a small pan, add ¼ tsp mustard seeds, a pinch of urad dal, and 4–5 curry leaves.
  • Add sprouts, toss for 2 minutes, then switch off and add 1–2 tbsp grated coconut and ½ tsp lemon juice.
  • Mix well and serve warm as a light South Indian-style sundal.
