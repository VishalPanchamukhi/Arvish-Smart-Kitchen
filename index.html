👩‍🍳 Simple cooking instructions

🥚 Eggs (omelette / bhurji)
  • Use non‑stick pan.
  • Heat ½ tsp ghee only, add onion/chilli → eggs → salt.
  • Cook on low flame.
🍗 Chicken curry (light)
  • Heat 1 tsp ghee, add jeera → onion → ginger‑garlic.
  • Add chicken + spices + water, cook covered (no frying).
🧀 Paneer stir fry
  • Pan heat → ½ tsp ghee.
  • Add paneer cubes + capsicum, toss lightly (no browning).
🌱 Sprouts / soaked grains
  • Soak overnight.
  • Pressure cook 1 whistle OR steam.
  • Mix lemon, onion, salt.
🌽 Sweet corn
  • Boil 5–7 minutes.
  • Add salt + pepper, no butter.
